# TVS King EV MAX - Product Knowledge Base

This document contains the official specifications, features, charging, warranty and support information for the TVS King EV MAX electric three-wheeler (electric auto rickshaw) from TVS Motor Company. Tagline: "Mushkil Raaste Ab Bane Aasaan" / "Aage Badho. Aaram Se."

## Overview

The TVS King EV MAX is an electric three-wheeler (e-auto / electric passenger rickshaw). It offers a certified range of 179 km on a full charge, a best-in-class top speed of 60 km/h, and fast charging from 0 to 80 percent in 2 hours 15 minutes. It is India's first connected three-wheeler, featuring TVS SmartXonnect with Bluetooth connectivity. The TVS King EV MAX comes with a comprehensive 6-year / 150,000 km warranty.

## Key Highlights of the TVS King EV MAX

- Certified range of 179 km per full charge.
- Best-in-class pickup and top speed of 60 km/h.
- Fast charging: 0 to 80 percent in 2 hours 15 minutes; 0 to 100 percent in 3 hours 30 minutes.
- India's first connected three-wheeler with TVS SmartXonnect (Bluetooth).
- LED headlamp and LED lights as standard.
- Comprehensive 6-year / 150,000 km warranty.
- Three drive modes: Eco, City and Power.
- Water-wading capability of 500 mm.

## Powertrain and Motor (TVS King EV MAX)

- **Motor type:** Permanent Magnet Synchronous Motor (PMSM)
- **Maximum power:** 11 kW
- **Maximum torque:** 40 Nm
- **Transmission:** 1 forward speed and 1 reverse speed
- **Drive modes:** 3 modes - Eco, City and Power

## Battery and Charging (TVS King EV MAX)

- **Battery type:** 51.2 V Lithium-Ion LFP (Lithium Iron Phosphate)
- **Battery capacity:** 9.2 kWh
- **Charger type:** Off-board charger with theft-proof protection
- **Charger capacity:** 3 kW
- **Charging time (0 to 100 percent):** 3 hours 30 minutes
- **Charging time (0 to 80 percent):** 2 hours 15 minutes

## Performance and Range (TVS King EV MAX)

- **Certified range:** 179 km on a full charge
- **Maximum speed:** 60 km/h
- **Top speed in Eco mode:** 40 km/h
- **Top speed in City mode:** 50 km/h
- **Top speed in Power mode:** 60 km/h
- **Gradeability:** 31 percent
- **Water-wading capability:** 500 mm

## Brakes, Suspension, Wheels and Tyres (TVS King EV MAX)

- **Brakes (front and rear):** Drum brakes, individually controlled on each wheel, foot operated
- **Front suspension:** Leading arm with coil spring
- **Rear suspension:** Hydraulic damper trailing arm with coil spring
- **Rim size (front and rear):** 3.5B x 12
- **Tyre size (front and rear):** 120/80 R12 6 PR, radial tubeless

## Dimensions and Capacity (TVS King EV MAX)

- **Wheelbase:** 2,000 mm
- **Wheel track:** 1,160 mm
- **Overall length:** 2,780 mm
- **Overall width:** 1,320 mm
- **Overall height:** 1,800 mm
- **Kerb weight:** 457 kg
- **Ground clearance (unladen):** 185 mm

## Colour Options (TVS King EV MAX)

The TVS King EV MAX is available in two colours: Pristine White and Neptune Blue.

## Connectivity - TVS SmartXonnect (TVS King EV MAX)

The TVS King EV MAX is India's first connected three-wheeler. It features TVS SmartXonnect with Bluetooth, allowing it to connect to the Apnaa TVS mobile app. The Apnaa TVS app can be downloaded from the Google Play Store and the Apple App Store.
